Why Choose Us

Engineering That Fits Ethiopian Industry

MEHON Design & Engineering aims to bridge the gap between engineering design and practical industrial applications. We provide technical expertise that helps businesses improve production efficiency, optimize machinery performance, and strengthen local manufacturing capabilities.

  • Strong Mechanical Design Expertise — complete machine and plant design from concept to fabrication drawings.
  • Understanding of Ethiopian Workshop Realities — designs matched to available fabrication methods.
  • Focus on Local Material Availability — material selection based on what can be sourced locally.
  • Experience in Agricultural & Industrial Machinery — from threshers and coffee hullers to full production plants.
  • Practical, Implementable Solutions — engineering knowledge, design methodology, and a problem-solving approach.
